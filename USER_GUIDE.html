User Guide

TTS Studio

Turn text into ready-to-ship voice prompts — one at a time, or a whole spreadsheet at once.

Overview

Two ways to work

Everything happens in your browser. Choose the mode that fits the job, set your voice once, and export.

Single

One clip

Type a line, generate it, add music if you want, and download a single audio file.

Bulk

A whole sheet

Upload an Excel or CSV and generate every row at once — each clip playable, editable, and packaged together.

Switch anytime using the Single Bulk control at the top.

Getting started

Open the app

Open your TTS Studio link in any modern browser. There's nothing to install. The page loads with Single mode ready to go.

Single mode

Make one clip

  1. Choose a voice engine

    Select ElevenLabs or Azure at the top of the Speech card.

  2. Enter your text

    Type or paste the words you want spoken into the text box.

  3. Set the voice

    For ElevenLabs, paste the Voice ID. For Azure, enter the voice name. Adjust the sliders — stability, speed, and the others — to taste.

  4. Generate and listen

    Press Generate Speech, then play the clip that appears to hear it.

  5. Add background music (optional)

    In the Background Music card, pick the provided track or upload your own, set the volume, and trim it to the part you want. Press Play selection to preview the trim.

  6. Preview the mix

    Press Preview mix to hear the voice and music together before you commit — nudge the volume live until it sounds right.

  7. Choose a format and export

    Pick an output format, name the file, then press Generate & Export and Download.

Bulk mode

Make many at once

  1. Switch to Bulk

    Select Bulk at the top. Your voice, music, and format settings carry over.

  2. Upload your spreadsheet

    Choose an .xlsx or .csv file. The app reads it and shows your columns.

  3. Map the columns

    Pick the Text column (what gets spoken) and the Filename column (what each file is named). Set a filename suffix and a package folder name if you'd like.

  4. Generate all

    Press Generate All. Clips render a few at a time and appear in the list — each one playable right away.

  5. Fix any line on the spot

    Not happy with a phrase? Edit the text on that row and press Regenerate to redo just that clip.

  6. Download the package

    Press Download all to get a single ready-to-ship package — a folder of your audio, bundled with its meta.xlsx sheet.

Reference

Output formats

Choose the one your system expects. Telephony systems usually want 8 kHz.

FormatBest for
PCM · 8 kHz · monoTelephony / IVR

A-law · 8 kHz · monoTelephony (G.711 A-law)
µ-law · 8 kHz · monoTelephony (G.711 µ-law)
The Download all package is ready to hand off as-is — the audio and its accompanying sheet are already organized inside the folder for you.

Good to know

Tips

  • Listen before you export. Preview the mix so the music sits under the voice, not over it.
  • Trim long tracks. Pick just the section of music you want; it loops neatly to match the speech.
  • Regenerate freely. In Bulk, you can rewrite any single line and redo only that clip.
  • Name as you go. The filename column and suffix decide exactly how each file is named in the package.
